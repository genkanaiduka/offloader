❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What camera media cards and external drives are supported? ▼
Offloader supports every media card and storage volume recognized by macOS. This includes CFexpress Type A & B, RED Mini-Mag, CFast 2.0, SD cards (UHS-II / UHS-I), and microSD cards. Output backup destinations include portable NVMe SSDs (Thunderbolt / USB4), hardware RAID 0/5/6 enclosures, external spinning hard drives, and network-attached storage (SMB / NAS).
Which checksum algorithm should I choose (xxHash64, MD5, SHA-256)? ▼
For High-Speed On-Set Production: We strongly recommend xxHash64. It delivers multi-gigabyte-per-second verification speed without throttling ultra-fast NVMe SSDs or Thunderbolt card readers, guaranteeing bit-exact byte-for-byte fidelity.

For Post-Production & Studio Deliverables: Select MD5 or SHA-256 if your film production insurance, streaming platform mandate (e.g., Netflix, Amazon Studios), or post-facility deliverable guidelines explicitly require standard cryptographic digests.
How do I resolve disk or card permission errors on macOS? ▼
macOS security features enforce granular permissions for external volumes:
1. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security on your Mac.
2. Scroll to Files and Folders.
3. Locate Offloader in the list and ensure Removable Volumes is toggled ON.
4. Relaunch Offloader to allow full read and write access to your connected media cards and backup drives.
Where are offload verification reports (PDF & Manifest) stored? ▼
Upon every completed offload, Offloader automatically generates an audit-ready PDF verification report and manifest directly in the root of each destination target folder. The report logs file counts, total bytes copied, transfer duration, average throughput, and bit-exact checksum hashes for every single clip.
