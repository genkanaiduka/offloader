Offloader

Privacy Policy for macOS

100% On-Device & Zero Data Collection

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Offloader (the "Application") is a professional media offloading and checksum verification application engineered specifically for cinematographers, DITs (Digital Imaging Technicians), photographers, and video editors on macOS.

The Application is built upon a fundamental architectural commitment: **Privacy by Design and Zero Data Collection**. We believe your camera media and production data are entirely yours. Offloader operates strictly on your local machine and does not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal data, footage, or operational logs to external servers.

🛡️ Executive Summary: Offloader performs 100% of its computation locally on your Mac. We do not collect, transmit, or monetize your personal information or media files.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The Application does not collect, record, or share any of the following categories of information:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, account credentials, or Apple IDs.
  • Media & Production Content: No video files (e.g., ProRes, ARRIRAW, REDCODE RAW, Blackmagic RAW), audio tracks, RAW still images, folder hierarchies, or file names.
  • Telemetry & Usage Analytics: No tracking SDKs, feature usage statistics, session recordings, or crash reports sent to third-party endpoints.
  • Device Identifiers & Geolocation: No IP addresses, device identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), hardware serial numbers, or location tracking.
  • Advertising & Marketing: Zero third-party advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or tracking cookies are embedded in the Application.

3. Local File Access & Security (macOS App Sandbox)

Offloader strictly complies with Apple's macOS **App Sandbox** security standards:

  • Explicit User Consent: The Application only accesses files, camera cards, and target storage volumes that you explicitly select via macOS system dialogs or direct drag-and-drop actions.
  • Pure Local Processing: Single-Read Multi-Write (SRMW) disk operations, multi-threaded checksum hashing (xxHash64, MD5, SHA-256), and verification report generation (PDF and manifest) execute entirely on your Mac's CPU and memory.

4. Network & Connectivity

Offloader does not connect to any internet or remote cloud servers. The Application uses a local loopback interface (localhost / 127.0.0.1) exclusively for high-speed inter-process communication between its native WebKit interface and local computation engine. This traffic is strictly internal and never leaves your computer.

5. Third-Party Services & Frameworks

The Application integrates zero third-party telemetry, analytics, or tracking services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or Meta/Facebook SDKs).

6. Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, and is in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy in future updates, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
